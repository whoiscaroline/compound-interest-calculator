The curves use a constant effective annual return, converted to a monthly rate. The outer lines change your assumed return by two percentage points. They are sensitivity scenarios, not probabilities or confidence intervals.

“Today's dollars” discounts future balances by your inflation assumption. Contributions stay fixed in nominal dollars. Taxes, fees, and market volatility are excluded. Real investing is less smooth than these lines.
